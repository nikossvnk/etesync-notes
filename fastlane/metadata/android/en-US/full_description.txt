Notes and bookmarks, end-to-end encrypted and synced with an Etebase server: your own (it's free and open source), or an EteSync account.

This is an independent fork of EteSync Notes. It's not affiliated with or supported by EteSync.

Notes
=====
* Write in Markdown, with a formatting bar for headings, bold, lists, checklists, quotes and links
* Checklists can be ticked right in the note
* Keep notes in notebooks, each with its own color, and filter the list by notebook
* Search all of your notes
* Select several notes to delete them at once
* Export all notes to a zip of Markdown files, and import them from one

Bookmarks
=========
* Save links from any app with "Save to EteSync" in the share menu, without leaving that app
* Add a title, a note and tags, and find them again by searching or by tag
* A link that's saved already is updated instead of added twice

Private
=======
* Everything is encrypted on your phone before it's sent: not even the server can read it
* Works offline, and syncs when the connection is back
* Images from the internet in notes are only loaded when you tap them
* Light and dark themes
* Free and open source, licensed under the GPL-3.0
